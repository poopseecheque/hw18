Small moments – read and notice

Salim: “The new regulations aim to create a level playing field for all companies in the industry. Finally, smaller businesses can compete fairly.”

Maria: “I am very sorry. I dropped the ball and failed to call the customer before it was too late. Now we’ve lost the account.”

Tom: “Our company is employing a full-court press to launch the new product successfully – extra shifts, non-stop meetings, the whole team is in.”

Elena: “She hit a grand slam in her presentation. Everyone, from the audience to the executive, was impressed. Complete victory.”

James: “If all else fails, we might have to resort to a Hail Mary pass and hope for a miracle. It’s our last chance.”

Lin: “When I'm in the zone, I can finish my work in record time and produce my best results. Hours pass like minutes.”

Anna: “He jumped the gun by announcing the project before finalizing the details. Now everyone is confused and angry.”

Kai: “I can't believe she agreed to go out with me. I always thought she was out of my league – way too talented and attractive.”

Omar: “If you would just play ball, we can get the assignment done on time. We need everyone to cooperate.”

Nina: “She threw a curveball by announcing her resignation right before the most important meeting of the year. Nobody saw it coming.”

Paul: “After struggling for years to make the business profitable, the owner finally decided to throw in the towel and close down. He just couldn’t keep going.”
