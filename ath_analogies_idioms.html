Exercise E – Speaking & discussion

No timer: speak naturally and expand your ideas with examples.

Personal speaking prompts

  • Describe a time you dropped the ball. What happened?
  • Tell me about a moment you were completely in the zone.
  • Have you ever made a Hail Mary pass attempt? Did it work?
  • Do you prefer a full-court press or a slow, careful strategy?
  • Talk about a time you threw in the towel – or refused to.
Try: drop the ballin the zoneHail Mary passfull-court pressthrow in the towel

Discussion questions

  • Is a level playing field possible in business? In education? In dating?
  • When is it smart to jump the gun, and when is it just a mistake?
  • Have you ever felt out of your league in a job, hobby, or social group?
  • What’s more valuable: the ability to throw a curveball or handle one?
level playing fieldjump the gunout of your leaguethrow a curveball

Controversial takes (Agree / Disagree / Complicate)

  • “If you drop the ball at work, you don’t deserve a second chance.”
  • “Throwing in the towel is not failure – it’s knowing when to stop.”
  • “In most situations, there is no level playing field. People who say otherwise are naive.”
  • “Playing ball with others usually means sacrificing your own goals.”
